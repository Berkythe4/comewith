As of 2026-08-19, Keith is the **sole owner** of Come With. Martin, Henry and
Janelle can each earn **5% by sweat equity over two years** (15% total), but none
has vested — today they are contractors only. Martin has never been paid in cash;
he is contributing labour toward the equity alone.

**Why:** this determines the tax filing. Single owner means the ~$28,000 2026 loss
should reach Keith's personal return and offset his 2026 lump sum. The moment any
equity vests, the entity likely becomes a partnership (Form 1065, K-1s) with a
different answer. Equity for services is also compensation on vesting, and a
§83(b) election — where available — must be filed within 30 days of grant. Grant
dates are not yet known and are time-sensitive.

**How to apply:** don't describe Come With as having co-founders or partners in
financial or tax output. Treat the entity question as answered (sole owner) but
flag the vesting conversion whenever multi-year projections or tax treatment come
up. See [[comewith-1099-tracking]] for the related contractor reporting.
