Keep Come With Radio captions plain, short and modest. On Ep 6 (2026-10-09) Keith
passed on several polished drafts ("pregame", "soundtrack for getting there", "a
big few weeks") with "we need to be modest and sound real", and then wrote the
line himself:

> Mixed by Berky with track contributions from Miss Vee. Check Vee out at
> Trans-Pecos on 10/23 and both Berky & Vee at Dance Infusion 11/14 @ Signal.

**Why:** the captions go out under the brand and next to real people's names; hype
reads as fake, and a claim like "every track" or "most" has to be literally true
(he checked the "100%" line against the data).

**How to apply:** credit who mixed and who contributed, name the upcoming shows with
date + venue, and stop. Use his Ep 5 shape (title line / one or two plain sentences /
"Come With & Enjoy!"). Offer 2-3 options in that register, not adjectives. Same text
goes on the site, SoundCloud and YouTube, plus hashtags (YouTube ignores all of them
past 15). Related: [[user-fpa-background]].
