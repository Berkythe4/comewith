## The arc

**Set out to:** tidy a station and make a page nicer.

**Discovered:** the page was serving **1,103 artists of which only 415 had any
music scanned**. 688 dead-end cards. No song search across 14,543 songs. The set
being built was in a collapsed `<details>` with no way to remove from it, and
every keystroke re-rendered the document, collapsing anything you had opened. The
design brief wrote itself once the real payload was opened — which is the lesson
(§69): a view spun out of an internal tool inherits its data and loses its
ergonomics, and nobody on the team has ever done the task on the page they hand
out.

**Then the date change turned into the real finding.** Anchoring the window on the
drop date was obviously right editorially. But it is fed by a *different* window —
scanning runs off the Build tab's range — so the corrected window contained 327
artists with **57** scanned. The date change was correct and would have shipped a
crate of 57 to a guest DJ. §70. That is now the top item in CARRYOVER, and it is
a ten-minute button press, not code.

**And the last question surfaced a latent bug.** "Does a reload update existing
links?" — yes, it is live-read, proved against the cache rather than asserted from
the code (13/13, 11/11, 16/16 songs, two rows scanned in July). But adding a
refresh button meant the array could change under the view, and expanded cards
were keyed by **index**. A refresh would have silently opened the wrong artist.
§71.

## Key decisions

- **Drop date, not today**, as the crate's anchor; explicit start still wins.
- **The DJ's typed show info goes to `comment`, never `show_date`/`show_venue`** —
  those publish as checked fact, and a missed show belongs in `ra_events` with
  `source='manual'`. §72. Flagged to Keith as reversible if he wants otherwise.
- **`sc_song_log` left alone** when clearing SHOW 10, so 46 songs stay marked
  carried and cannot reappear. Flagged as his call, not taken silently.
- **No second renderer, no second copy of the scan count.** 🎯 Point the artist
  window here reuses the existing "↻ Refresh music & data (N)" rather than
  recomputing coverage in the modal.
