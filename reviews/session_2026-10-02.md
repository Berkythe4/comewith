## Choices the spec didn't cover
- Secret in the **path** (headers are beta in claude.ai); `x-api-key` also accepted.
- Legacy stages: `review→ready`, `planned→scheduled` for live rows; `archived` kept as a
  legal, labelled, unoffered stage.
- New posts made in the dashboard default their owner to **Janelle** (same as the
  connector) so Approve always notifies someone; approving your own post notifies nobody.
- **Use this** never overwrites a non-empty, different Final on one click — a second
  click within 2s confirms.
- Notes from the connector: new `author_name` column ("Claude"), `author_id` null.
- Series / Channel filter rows only appear while a value is picked (the fields are
  hidden); a **🤖 Needs review** shortcut chip was added beside "Not posted".
- `add_note` is allowed on approved posts (a note changes nothing on the post).
- Ranges are New York days; list/get_results cap at 120 days.
- The E2E's bell insert runs in a rolled-back transaction so Janelle is not pinged by
  a test; the dashboard's `notify()` path is unchanged.
