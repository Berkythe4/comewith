What a free account gets you

Come With Radio is a monthly mix of artists who are about to play in New York. The account is free, takes one tap, and there's no password.

  1. ♡ Tap the heart on any songIt goes into your playlist. That's the whole thing — no folders, no setup.
  2. Your playlist is always hereOpen ♡ My playlist at the top of any page. Every song keeps its link to SoundCloud, so you can go play it, follow the artist, or buy it.
  3. We tell you when your artists play New YorkSave a song, and when that artist books a NYC show, it shows up in your playlist with the date, venue, price and ticket link.
  4. Take it with you, anytimeExport your saved songs — or any episode's full tracklist — as a spreadsheet with BPM and key. Works in Rekordbox, Serato and Excel.
  5. Sort it like a DJOrder your saves by BPM or Camelot key when you're planning a set.

We never post anything, never share your email, and never charge for this. Unsubscribe or delete anytime.
