Who we are

Come With is an independent DJ and event production operation based in Brooklyn, NY. Founded by Berky (Keith Berkman) — DJ, producer, and event organizer with roots in the New York house music community.

We DJ and produce private events — dinner parties, brunches, club nights, and corporate events. We also throw Come With, our namesake house-music party series, and produce Dance Infusion, our brunch dance event series benefiting the National MS Society's Emerging Leaders Alliance.
