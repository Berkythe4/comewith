# Dance Infusion Series Summary

Dance Infusion is a Come With production. It is a house-music event series partnered with the National MS Society's Emerging Leaders Alliance, raising funds for MS research and awareness.

## Series mission
Combine community, dance, and fundraising. Each event is measured both as a normal event (P&L) and as a fundraiser (total raised, cost-per-dollar-raised).
