Come With NYC Radio Ep8, mixed by guest DJ Kristen London.

I’ve always loved getting problems to solve, especially when those problems come in neat little boxes. So when Keith came to me with the Come Here prompt, I was actually pretty excited to take it on. For this mix, I could only choose tracks from artists that were playing in NYC within a month of the release date. When I took my first look at November’s lineups, unsurprisingly, promoters booked mostly indie and new wave artists (duh, fall). These are genres that are way out of my comfort zone, but I had an absolute blast using Keith’s platform to discover new artists and create the mix.

Take a listen - I’m proud of what came out of this one.

Catch them live in NYC: Avalon Emerson at Music Hall of Williamsburg (11/5), Eli Escobar at House of Yes (11/6), Kelly Lee Owens at Good Room (11/6), Roza Terenzi and Alex Kassian at BASEMENT (11/6), Kim Ann Foxman and Zombies In Miami at The Chocolate Factory (11/7), DJ Harvey at Knockdown Center (11/13), Laurel Halo at Nowadays (11/20), Jen Cardini at Public Records (11/29) and more.
