Dance Infusion · benefiting the National MS Society

A dancefloor
with a purpose.

We started Dance Infusion because someone we love lives with MS. Each event is a curated night where the proceeds move toward the National MS Society — and we publish exactly how much reaches the mission.

$1,140

Dance Infusion #1 → the MS Society

42 tickets, solo-run proof of concept — 39% to the mission.

117

Dance Infusion #2 → in the room

A bigger night at Signal; ~$3,000 to the cause.

Partner on a benefit
"We don't ask you to donate and walk away. We ask you to come dance — and the night does the rest."— Berky, founder of Come With
Transparent by design

Every Dance Infusion publishes its raised total and "% to mission". No black box — that's how a benefit earns a community's trust, and the next ticket.

How we work

A studio approach to a night out.

i.

Curate

Lineup, sound, and room chosen for the crowd — not the lowest common denominator.

ii.

Produce

We own the technical: CDJs, sound, lighting, load-in, run-of-show. The rig shows up and works.

iii.

Host

A real host keeps the energy and the moments — the toast, the drop, the room.

iv.

Capture & report

Recap content for the next flyer; for benefits, a published "% to mission".
