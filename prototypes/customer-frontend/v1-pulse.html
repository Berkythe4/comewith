Dance Infusion turns a great night out into real support for people living with multiple sclerosis. Every ticket, every round, every donation moves the needle.

How it works

A Dance Infusion night = a curated lineup + a real room + a clear "% to mission." We publish exactly what we raised and what reached the National MS Society — no black box.

DI #1 → $1,140 to the MS Society (39% to mission). DI #2 → 117 in the room, ~$3,000 to the cause.
