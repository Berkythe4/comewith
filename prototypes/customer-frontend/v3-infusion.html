Why we do this

This one's personal.

Someone we love lives with multiple sclerosis. MS is unpredictable, exhausting, and isolating — and the community around it is everything. So we built Dance Infusion: a night where showing up is the support.

Every Dance Infusion benefits the National MS Society, and we publish exactly what we raised and what reached the mission — because trust is how a benefit keeps going.

— Berky, founder of Come With
