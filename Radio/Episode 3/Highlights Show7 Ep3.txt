Highlights S7 E3

Finding some new nasty dub step beats is always rewarding. I'm pretty familiar with artists in this genre but Casey Club's track Boreholw was a new discovery for me and it gets downright grimy!

Pop Pop by Channel Tres is a definite earworm. Chill vibe with a nice clean beat and smooth lyrics that get you moving and dancing before you realize what happened. 

Chris Luno's track Between This comes through with a dirty bassline and euphoric hi notes that just feel so right together. Listen, relax and have your vibes tuned in the best way.
